# QMS Precast
## New Company Onboarding Checklist

**Document Type:** Standard Operating Procedure  
**Purpose:** Create, verify, and launch a new company inside QMS Precast using the approved Google Drive folder structure.  
**Used By:** QMS Precast Administrators

---

## 1. Pre-Onboarding Check

Before setting up a new company, confirm the following:

- Company name
- Main contact
- Reporting contact
- Audit recipients
- Overdue alert recipients
- Company is approved to go live

---

## 2. Standard Google Drive Locations

Use the following structure:

- Root folder: `QMS Precast Root`
- Template folder: `00 Master Control/Company Template - COPY ME`
- Live company folder location: `01 Live Companies`

---

## 3. Create The Company Folder

Complete these steps in order:

1. Open `Company Template - COPY ME`
2. Make a copy of the template folder
3. Rename the copied folder to `QMS - [Company Name]`
4. Move the copied folder into `01 Live Companies`

---

## 4. Rename The Company Files

Inside the copied company folder, rename:

- `Company Onboarding Record` to `[Company Name] - Onboarding Record`
- `Company Contacts` to `[Company Name] - Contacts`
- `Audit Responses` to `[Company Name] - Audit Responses`

---

## 5. Check The Folder Structure

Confirm the company folder contains:

- `01 Onboarding`
- `02 Audit Forms`
- `03 Response Sheets`
- `04 Evidence`
- `05 Exports`
- `06 Admin Notes`

Do not rename or remove the numbered folder structure.

---

## 6. Check Audit Forms

Open `02 Audit Forms` and confirm the correct audit set is in place.

Standard forms:

- `HS Audit - General`
- `HS Audit - PPE`
- `HS Audit - Housekeeping`
- `HS Audit - Equipment`

Add or remove company-specific forms only if required.

---

## 7. Complete The Master Onboarding Form

1. Submit the master onboarding form for the new company
2. Confirm the new company appears in the onboarding responses sheet
3. Check the submitted company details match the folder being created

---

## 8. Set Up The Company In QMS Precast

1. Sign in as `admin`
2. Open `Admin`
3. Press `Onboard new company`
4. Connect Google if needed
5. Refresh the company folder list
6. Select the new company folder

Then verify:

- Onboarding form
- Audit forms
- Response sheet

When all three are verified:

1. Press `Sync company`
2. Confirm the company status changes to live

---

## 9. Create Audit Schedules

Open `Schedule setup` and create the required schedules.

For each schedule, set:

- Schedule type: company or personal
- Frequency
- Send time
- Recipients
- Overdue alert recipients
- Report-to contact
- Completion check timing

Use personal schedules only where an audit must be assigned to a specific individual.

---

## 10. Go-Live Check

Before completion, confirm:

- Correct company folder selected
- Onboarding form verified
- Audit forms verified
- Response sheet verified
- Company synced
- Required schedules created

---

## 11. Operating Rules

- One folder per company
- One response sheet per company
- Do not mix two companies in one folder
- Keep audit forms inside `02 Audit Forms`
- Keep evidence inside `04 Evidence`
- Keep exports inside `05 Exports`
- Keep the numbered structure unchanged

---

## 12. Completion Standard

A company is considered live when:

- Folder is created
- Onboarding form is recorded
- Forms are verified
- Response sheet is verified
- Company is synced
- Schedules are created
