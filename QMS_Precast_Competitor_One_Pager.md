# QMS Precast Competitor One-Pager

## Health & Safety Event 2026

This shortlist focuses on the exhibitors most similar to QMS Precast's current product direction: health and safety audits, inspections, scheduling, corrective actions, mobile completion, and centralised reporting.

## Closest Competitors

### EcoOnline
- Strong overlap on audits, inspections, recurring checks, corrective actions, and centralised safety data.
- More established and enterprise-scale, but broader and heavier to position.
- QMS Precast angle: simpler setup, clearer Google-folder workflow, easier dedicated-tablet story.

### Evotix
- Very close overlap on audit templates, mobile inspections, evidence capture, scheduling, and actions.
- Strong compliance and operations message with a mature EHS platform.
- QMS Precast angle: easier for site teams to understand and faster to get live.

### HSI Donesafe
- Broad EHS platform with audit and inspection management, compliance, risk, and actions.
- Powerful but positioned as a larger all-in-one platform.
- QMS Precast angle: more focused, more operational, less platform complexity.

### Notify Technology
- Strong match on audits, inspections, checklists, recurring schedules, and mobile completion.
- Close in day-to-day workflow language.
- QMS Precast angle: stronger company-specific onboarding and clearer one-company-per-tablet setup.

### Vatix
- Similar on audits and inspections, mobile use, corrective actions, and health and safety workflows.
- Broad H&S platform positioning.
- QMS Precast angle: more structured around folder setup, audit rollout, and company-specific use.

### Benchmark Gensuite
- Strong inspection lifecycle and follow-up action story.
- More enterprise-heavy than QMS Precast.
- QMS Precast angle: more accessible, easier to adopt, simpler to explain on a stand.

## Second-Tier Similar Exhibitors

- Ideagen
- VinciWorks
- Bizzmine
- Q-Hub Compliance Software

These are relevant but feel broader, more enterprise-led, or less clearly field-audit-first than the strongest competitors above.

## Show Positioning For QMS Precast

- Easier to deploy than large enterprise EHS suites.
- Better story for dedicated tablets and live site use.
- Clear company onboarding and company-folder structure.
- Practical audit rollout rather than broad platform complexity.
- Simpler operational workflow for teams who need to get moving quickly.

1. EcoOnline
2. Evotix
3. HSI Donesafe
4. Notify Technology
